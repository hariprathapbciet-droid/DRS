About Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy is an eye condition that can occur in people with diabetes. High blood sugar levels can damage the small blood vessels in the retina, which is the light-sensitive part at the back of the eye. In the early stages, there may be no noticeable symptoms. Regular eye examinations can help detect changes early and help protect vision.
